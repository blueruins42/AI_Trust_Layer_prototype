## Page 1: Product Overview

The ZDJ-200 electric switch machine is an electric device used to move and lock railway turnouts (switches), suitable for 50kg/m and 60kg/m rails. It is widely used in urban rail transit and national railway networks.

## Page 12: Installation and Commissioning

Switch machine installation should ensure:
1. The switch machine installation device is tightly fitted to the stock rail
2. The gap between the switch blade and stock rail does not exceed 4mm
3. Switching force testing should be conducted separately under no-load and loaded conditions
4. Operating time testing should be conducted at rated voltage
