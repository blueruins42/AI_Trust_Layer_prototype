## Page 10: Signaling System Cost Reference

Signaling system cost reference ranges (per line length):
- CBTC system: 8-12 million CNY/km
- Point-based ATP system: 5-7 million CNY/km
- Interlocking system (standalone): 2-3.5 million CNY/station
